Climate & energy. Designed together.

Generate. Store. Control. Use efficiently.

Solar panels can generate electricity when the sun is available. Battery storage can retain surplus generation for later use. Efficient heat pumps and HVAC systems can then make smarter use of that energy.

Rather than treating heating, cooling, solar and storage as separate technologies, JJ ACR looks at how they can work together as one integrated energy strategy.

Generate→Store→Control→Consume

A more intelligent building

Reduce imported energy, make better use of on-site generation and support efficient heating and cooling.
